## Overview

This entry groups two collaborative academic resources that sit
alongside, but are distinct from, the authored research software in
this portfolio: a supervised thesis project on critical discourse
analysis, and a conceptual metaphor database co-developed in the
context of a research project I co-directed. They are included here as
collaborative research resources rather than as standalone authored
software products.

## Research purpose

Supervised and collaborative work is part of how research methods in
this area travel: through theses, seminars, and shared databases.
Documenting these resources publicly makes that contribution traceable
without conflating supervision and co-direction with authorship of
software.

## Contribution

- A supervised thesis project (`acd-vbg`), forked from the original
  author's repository, included as a record of supervised academic work.
- A conceptual metaphor database / wiki developed within a research
  project I co-directed, used as a shared reference for collaborators
  and as a resource for further annotation and analysis.

These are surfaced here so the relationship to the rest of the
portfolio is explicit: they sit next to the authored research software
without being presented as authored software themselves.

## Status

Collaborative reference resources. Maintenance follows the lifecycle of
the original projects rather than an independent release schedule.

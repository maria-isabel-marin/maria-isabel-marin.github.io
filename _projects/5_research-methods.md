## Overview

A pair of resources gathered under a single research-methods entry:
the **Research Lab** repository, which collects research materials and
documentation connected to publications and ongoing computational
linguistics work, and a **Spanish metaphor annotation method** —
a systematised instrument for the identification, annotation, and
analysis of conceptual metaphors in Spanish corpora.

## Research purpose

Spanish-language metaphor research lacks a shared, replicable instrument
for moving from text to annotated conceptual metaphors. The annotation
method here is based on the MIPVU protocol and has been refined through
research projects in cognitive linguistics and discourse analysis,
so that decisions made during annotation remain traceable and
comparable across studies.

## Contribution

- A systematised annotation instrument grounded in MIPVU and adapted to
  Spanish-language conceptual metaphor research.
- A research-lab repository that consolidates methodological documents
  and data-related resources tied to specific publications and projects,
  so they can be cited and reused rather than scattered across private
  notes.
- A connection point between the methodological work and the software
  ecosystem: annotation decisions made here inform the schemas used by
  [Metaphor Manager](/projects/2_metaphor-manager/) and the
  [MetaphorModel.org](/projects/1_metaphormodel/) data model.

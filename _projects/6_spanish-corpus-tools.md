## Overview

A set of **Spanish-focused corpus and linguistic-annotation tools**
developed alongside teaching and research in computational linguistics at
the Universidad de Antioquia and in collaboration with the GES
sociolinguistics group. The work emphasises low-resource and
research-driven contexts rather than off-the-shelf NLP pipelines.

## Research problem

Standard NLP stacks are heavily oriented towards English. Spanish
metaphor and discourse research — particularly when it involves
non-standard registers, regional varieties, or specific genres — needs
tooling that can be adjusted to a corpus and an annotation scheme
rather than tooling that assumes the corpus matches the model. The
challenge is methodological as much as engineering: what counts as a
conceptual metaphor expression in a given corpus, and how should that
decision be encoded in an annotator?

## What it provides

- A tagger for conceptual metaphor expressions (CEMs) in Spanish text.
- Corpus and annotation tooling developed in the Universidad de
  Antioquia research context.
- Companion resources and analyses for conceptual metaphor work in
  Spanish, used alongside published linguistic studies.

## Research software contribution

The contribution is in adapting computational methods to Spanish
metaphor research and in supporting collaborative and supervised work:
graduate research projects, coursework, and joint research with the GES
group. The tools are intentionally lightweight and scriptable so that
they can be reused as starting points for further annotation work in
Spanish or in similar low-resource settings, and so that methodological
decisions stay visible rather than hidden behind an opaque pipeline.

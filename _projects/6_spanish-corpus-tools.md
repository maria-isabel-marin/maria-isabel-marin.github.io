A set of **Spanish-focused corpus and linguistic-annotation tools**
developed alongside teaching and research in computational linguistics at
the Universidad de Antioquia and in collaboration with the GES
sociolinguistics group. The work emphasises **low-resource and
research-driven contexts** rather than off-the-shelf NLP pipelines.

## Repositories

- [`CEM_tagger`](https://github.com/maria-isabel-marin/CEM_tagger) — tagger for conceptual metaphor expressions (CEMs) in Spanish text
- [`exmachina-udea`](https://github.com/maria-isabel-marin/exmachina-udea) — corpus and annotation tooling developed in the UdeA research context
- [`MetaforasConceptuales`](https://github.com/maria-isabel-marin/MetaforasConceptuales) — companion resources and analyses for conceptual metaphor work in Spanish

## What it's for

- Annotating and tagging Spanish corpora for downstream metaphor and
  discourse analysis
- Supporting research projects and graduate work where standard
  English-oriented NLP stacks fall short
- Providing reproducible artefacts to accompany published linguistic studies

## Engineering profile

- Lightweight, scriptable tools rather than full applications
- Aligned with research outputs in Spanish-language venues (see
  [publications](/publications/))
- Suitable as a starting point for further annotation work in Spanish or in
  similar low-resource settings

## Status

Mixed. Some components are stable enough for reuse in coursework and
research projects; others are experimental and document a specific
methodology. Refer to each repository's README for current status.
